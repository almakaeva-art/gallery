About me

My love for flowers began at art school, on visits to the botanical garden where we painted bright dahlias and the feathery blooms of astilbe.

Years later it grew into my own floral studio, first in Saint Petersburg and then in sunny Marbella. Working with fresh flowers every day taught me their character and their ever-changing rhythms, and painting them became a natural next step.

I’m less interested in copying a flower than in capturing the feeling it gives.

I paint in oil and acrylic and let each canvas find its own way. I hope my paintings bring a little beauty and emotion into your everyday life.
