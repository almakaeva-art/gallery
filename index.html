About the art

A lifelong love of flowers

My love for flowers began many years ago, at art school, with visits to the botanical garden to paint bright dahlias and the feathery blooms of astilbe.

Years later it became my own floral studio, first in Saint Petersburg and later in sunny Marbella. Working with fresh flowers every day taught me their character, natural beauty and ever-changing rhythms. Botanical painting became a natural extension of that passion.

I am less interested in simply reproducing a flower than in capturing the feeling it evokes.

I work in oil and acrylic, letting each painting develop in its own way. Through my art, I hope to bring beauty and emotion into everyday life.
